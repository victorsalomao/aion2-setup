Crafting in AION 2 can take a weapon or armor piece to item level 102, the same ceiling as the raid. The top recipes need dungeon materials, though, so crafting works alongside dungeons, not instead of them.

To look up any recipe, use the [recipe database](/en/tools/recipes/). To plan the Dragon Lord staff, ring or guard with a shopping list, use the [Dragon Lord crafting menu](/en/tools/craft/).

## The short version

- **Five professions:** Blacksmithing, Armorsmithing, Handicrafting, Alchemy and Cooking. Gathering is a separate skill, Essence Extraction.
- **512 recipes** in the global client, 256 per faction. Elyos and Asmodians have mirrored lists: different names, same numbers.
- **Mastery from 1 to 100.** It stops at 50 until you finish the profession's upgrade quest.
- **No recipe charges Kinah.** The only cost is materials.
- **The combo is the path.** 25% of gear crafts come out Splendent, and that is what the next recipe asks for.

## The professions and what they make

| Profession | Recipes per faction | What it makes |
|---|---|---|
| Blacksmithing | 52 | Greatsword, longsword, dagger, mace and guard; Orichalcum Ingots and Balaur horns |
| Armorsmithing | 72 | All seven armor slots (chest, legs, helm, pauldrons, gloves, boots, cloak); Balaur leather |
| Handicrafting | 63 | Bow, staff, necklace, earrings, ring and bracelet; gem decorations and Balaur scales |
| Alchemy | 52 | Spellbook and orb, potions, serums, scrolls, Manastones and Soulstones; powders and paper |
| Cooking | 17 | Buff food, up to the White Dragon dishes (Dark Dragon on the Asmodian side) |

The game files call Armorsmithing and Handicrafting *Tailoring* and *Jewelcrafting*. A few basic materials come from more than one profession: Orichalcum Ingot from Blacksmithing and Armorsmithing, Magic Crystal from Alchemy and Handicrafting.

### Which one to pick

Your class weapon decides most of it:

- **Blacksmithing:** Gladiator (greatsword), Templar (longsword), Assassin (dagger) and Cleric (mace), plus the guard.
- **Handicrafting:** Ranger (bow) and Chanter (staff).
- **Alchemy:** Sorcerer (spellbook) and Spiritmaster (orb).

If your weapon is not the goal, Armorsmithing is the safest bet: it has the longest list and all seven pieces fit any class. Handicrafting is a close second, since necklaces, earrings and rings are class-neutral too. Alchemy is the utility pick: Intermediate Manastone at mastery 20 and Superior at 85, and everyone sockets Manastones. Cooking has the shortest list and no gear; it works best as a second profession.

## Where the stations are

Every profession has its station in the faction's main field map:

- **Elyos:** Dawn Legion Base and Zumion Village, in Verteron.
- **Asmodians:** Safe Haven and Nornir Assembly, in Altgard.

Recipes unlock on their own as mastery rises. The standard list has no recipe books.

## Mastery: how it rises and where it stops

Mastery runs from 1 to 100 in two ranks: Novice (1 to 50) and Professional (51 to 100). Every craft gives experience, and the top half is much longer: about 8,800 points from 1 to 50, then another 82,700 from 51 to 100. Professional costs roughly nine times as much as Novice.

### The upgrade quest at 50

At 50 the bar stops. Take your profession's upgrade quest (for example *Upgrade: Blacksmithing Specialty Skill*), craft the test item and hand it in. The reward lifts the cap to 100.

| Profession | Test item (mastery 50) |
|---|---|
| Blacksmithing | Novice Blacksmith's Hammer: 6 Orichalcum Ingot and 3 Odyle |
| Armorsmithing | Novice Armorsmith's Fabric: 6 Orichalcum Ingot and 3 Odyle |
| Handicrafting | Novice Handicrafter's Magnifying Glass: 4 Yggdrasil Wood (Asvata Wood), 2 Sapphire Decoration and 3 Odyle |
| Alchemy | Novice Alchemist's Scale: Magic Crystal, 2 Spiritstone Powder and 3 Odyle |
| Cooking | Novice Chef's Seasoning: 10 Fresh Raw Meat, 6 Fresh Fruit and 4 Inina (Conide) |

The Alchemy scale is listed with 6 Magic Crystal on the Elyos side and 4 on the Asmodian side. Bring 6 to be safe.

At mastery 100 each profession has its Professional rank item, which asks for an Artisan's Splendent piece and a pile of Balaur materials.

## Success, failure and combo

### Success chance

Crafting fills two gauges at once, one for success and one for failure. If the success gauge fills first, it works; if the failure gauge fills first, or you get attacked mid-craft, it fails.

Every recipe shows a range in the [recipe database](/en/tools/recipes/). The first number is the chance at the recipe's exact mastery; the second is the ceiling once your mastery is well past it:

| Recipe type | At exact mastery | Ceiling |
|---|---|---|
| Materials (ingots, powders, paper, Balaur parts) | 95.2% | 99.2% |
| Basic gear (Orichalcum, first jewelry) | 74% | 93.1% |
| Expert gear | 76.8% | 94.6% |
| Artisan gear | 79.6% | 96.8% |
| True Dragon Lord | 67.5% | 94.6% |
| Potions, scrolls and food | 72.9% | 85% to 91% |

In practice: Dragon Lord is both the riskiest and the most expensive recipe in the game. Craft it with mastery to spare, away from monsters.

### Combo and Splendent pieces

When a craft succeeds, it can still trigger a combo (great success). The combo result replaces the normal one:

- **25%** on most gear and potions;
- **30%** on Cooking;
- **20%** on some Manastone recipes.

On gear, the combo is not a bonus, it is the ladder. Orichalcum Longsword (Common, item level 18) becomes Splendent Orichalcum Longsword (Rare, 22) on a combo. The next recipe, Expert's, asks for exactly that Splendent piece. And so on: Expert's Splendent feeds Artisan's, and Artisan's Splendent feeds Dragon Lord. At 25%, expect 4 crafts per Splendent piece on average.

**Never sell or salvage a Splendent piece by accident.** It is the ticket to the next tier.

### Upgrade recipes

190 recipes need no mastery and show no success chance: they are upgrades. They take a piece you already own up one tier. In the Dragon Lord line, for example, True becomes Splendent True through an upgrade (11 Thick horns and 4 Wrathful Mind for the sword) if the combo does not land. White and Wise are upgrades too, and they consume another Artisan's Splendent piece on top of a lot of dungeon materials.

## Gathering and materials

### Essence Extraction

Gathering has 27 node types, all open at mastery 1. Ore, gem, wood and Odyle nodes roll four grades from the same node (base, Fine, Pure and Radiant); herbs roll two (base and Fresh); cooking ingredients one.

Gathering mastery pays points into a 21-skill tree. Each node group has three:

- **Seeker:** shows that node type on the minimap. Take the one for your most-used material first; it saves the most time.
- **Plentiful Harvest:** a chance to gather double, up to 30% at max rank.
- **Appraiser:** a chance to reroll the grade, which means more Pure and Radiant.

Gathering also stops at 50. The upgrade test (*Upgrade: Essence Extraction Specialty Skill*, unlocked at level 10 and recommended at 45) asks you to extract Ruby until you get a Splendent Ruby. Gathering levels far faster than crafting: about 2,100 points to 50 and 5,100 more to 100.

Verteron and Altgard hold about two thirds of each faction's nodes. That is where to level gathering.

### Where each material comes from

| Type | Examples | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Gathered | Orichalcum Ore, gems, Odyle, logs, herbs, ingredients | Essence Extraction nodes |
| Vendor reagent (Bound) | Orichalcum Solvent, Zircon Hammer, Professional Ink, Glass Bottle | Merchant; cheap, but not tradeable |
| Field drop | Refining Stone up to Artisan's Refining Stone, Spiritstone, Raw Leather, Fresh Raw Meat | Field monsters |
| Dungeon material | Artisan's Ultimate Refining Stone, Wrathful Mind, Will and Ego, Balaur's Essence | Party dungeons and the Ludra raid (Balaur's Essence also drops from some field monsters) |

The last row sets your pace. Ultimate Refining Stone and the Wrathful line do not drop in the field, so the Unique tiers are gated behind dungeons.

Many intermediate materials are crafted: ingots, powders, paper, decorations and the Balaur parts (horn, scale and leather, at mastery 51, 65 and 80). Each Balaur part takes 2 Balaur's Essence and a vendor reagent. The True Dragon Lord Longsword alone takes 22 horns, so 44 Balaur's Essence. In the [recipe database](/en/tools/recipes/), tap an item to see the whole tree down to raw materials.

## Mastery route

The example is Blacksmithing; the other gear professions follow the same shape, with mastery shifting a few levels between slots.

1. **Gather from day one.** Every node opens at mastery 1. Pick up ore, Odyle and gems along your quest routes.
2. **1 to 15: materials and the first tier.** Orichalcum Ingot at 1 (2 ore and 1 solvent), Orichalcum weapons at 3 and the guard at 5. The ingot is cheap, 95% safe and keeps the bar moving.
3. **15 to 30: Expert.** Expert's at 15 (guard at 20), each asking for the Splendent from the tier below. At 25 the batch ingot opens (10 at once).
4. **30 to 50: Artisan.** Artisan's at 30 (guard at 35). Keep every Artisan's Splendent piece: it is worth a True Dragon Lord, the White and Wise upgrades and the rank 100 item.
5. **50: upgrade quest.** Craft the test item and unlock the Professional rank.
6. **51 to 80: Balaur parts.** Thick horn at 51, Hard at 65 and Durable at 80. They are 95% safe and you will need dozens, so they are the natural way to climb the long bar.
7. **55 and 60: Dragon Lord.** True opens at 55 (weapons, ring, chest, legs, helm and pauldrons) and at 60 (guard, necklace, earrings, gloves, boots and cloak).

Do not chase 100 for gear. The last mastery-gated gear recipe is at 60; past that, mastery unlocks materials (Durable horn at 80) and the rank item. If you only want your own weapon, **80 is the practical target**.

## What is worth crafting at each stage

| Item level | Crafted | Dungeon or raid |
|---|---|---|
| 44 | Artisan's Splendent (combo at mastery 30) | — |
| 54 | — | Krao Cave, Draupnir |
| 62 | True Dragon Lord (mastery 55) | — |
| 70 | Splendent True Dragon Lord | Vakron Sky Island, Urugugu Canyon |
| 78 | White Dragon Lord (upgrade) | — |
| 86 | Splendent White Dragon Lord | Ferocious Horn Den, Fire Temple |
| 94 | Wise Dragon Lord (upgrade) | — |
| 102 | Splendent Wise Dragon Lord | Ludra raid (weapons and guard) |

- **Leveling (up to 45):** basic and Expert gear falls behind fast. Craft for mastery and to bank Splendent pieces, not to wear.
- **Fresh 45:** the Artisan's Splendent piece (44) is a step, not a destination. Krao Cave and Draupnir give 54.
- **Mid gear:** True Dragon Lord (62) already beats the 54 dungeons, and Splendent True (70) ties Vakron.
- **Endgame:** White (78) loses to Fire Temple (86); only Splendent White ties it. Wise (94) beats it, and Splendent Wise (102) is the top. Without raid access, crafting is the only way to item level 102 armor and accessories.
- **Amplification:** Splendent pieces at 70 and 86 only reach +1 and +3 Amplified, against +3 and +5 for dungeon gear of the same level. White (78) and Wise (94) reach +3 and +5, and Splendent Wise +5.
- **Always worth it:** consumables you burn every day (potions, scrolls, the White Dragon dishes at mastery 60, 75 and 90) and Manastones.

Verdict: crafting pays off for one or two slots that refuse to drop, usually your weapon, and for consumables. It does not replace dungeons; it eats their materials.
