A Unique card adds +80 gear score and lets you choose its type and one skill. It does not have to drop: you can craft it.

## The recipe

- **5 Mysterious Crystals and 2 million Kinah** for a card with 1 chosen skill.
- 15 crystals and 5 million for 2 skills, which is out of reach early on.
- One craft every 7 days.

## Where crystals come from

| Source | How many | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Tower of Trials achievement | up to 5 | One per score milestone in the Ascension Trial |
| Season Shop | up to 5 per character | 25,000 Oath Coin each |
| Arcana extraction | varies | Fills a bar; better and enhanced cards fill more |

No other season challenge achievement pays crystals, and there is no record of a direct drop.

## The Ascension Trial decides how many you get

The achievement counts your **best run**, not the sum. On Hard the score is 2,100 for a deathless clear, plus 3 points per second left on the clock.

| Milestone | Time that must be left on Hard |
|---|---|
| 1,000 | any deathless clear |
| 3,000 | 5:00 |
| 4,000 | 10:34 |
| 5,000 | 16:07 |

In practice Hard yields 3 crystals. The 5,000 and 6,000 milestones belong to Challenge, which needs 2,500 gear score. The common route is 3 from the Trial and 2 from the shop.

## What to pick

- **Type:** start with the Chalice, the hardest one to drop as Unique.
- **Skill:** your main damage skill.

> The formula and milestones come from Korean-version guides. Check the values on the achievement screen.
