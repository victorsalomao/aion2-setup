At 45 your level stops and the game starts measuring your character by gear score, shown in game as *item level*. Each band below is cheaper than the next one and unlocks it.

## Up to 1,150: clear what is permanent

Nothing here depends on luck or on a group.

1. Finish the main story. The last quest gives a Unique weapon.
2. Complete the level 45 Ascension.
3. Equip the Clash Rune, from a green quest unlocked at 45.
4. Clear the Sealed Dungeons and Strongholds on your map.
5. Take your region's Monolith to level 30 and spend the scrolls on the Revelation Amulet.
6. Spend what is sitting idle: Daevanion points, skill levels, all 4 Stigma slots and your pet.
7. Only then enhance: weapon to +10, then Amulet and Belt.

## 1,150 to 1,400: first Conquests

Krao Cave and Draupnir open at item level 700, but before 1,150 the run is slow and the cube pays little.

Krao Cave and Draupnir on Conquest drop item level 54 gear. Kill the mid bosses before the last one: they improve the cube reward.

## 1,400 to 1,600: group dungeons

Vakron Sky Island drops item level 70 armor and Urugugu Canyon drops weapons and accessories. Every 21 cubes in the same dungeon you pick a guaranteed reward.

## 1,600 to 1,900: first Arcana cards

Transcendence opens at 1,600. There are 5 slots, one per card type, and an empty slot is the worst case. Stage 1 cards usually come as Common, +20 each.

## 1,900 to 2,200: stage 2 and item level 86 gear

- **1,900:** Transcendence stage 2, with +40 cards.
- **2,000:** Ascension Trial on Hard.
- **2,100:** Fire Temple (weapons and accessories) and Ferocious Horn Den (armor), with item level 86 gear.

Once you pass 2,100, stop crafting item level 70 pieces. A level 70 accessory at +10 reaches an effective 90; a level 86 one at +10 reaches 106.

## 2,200 and beyond

Transcendence stage 3 at 2,200 and stage 4 at 2,500. The Sanctuary, the 10-player raid, opens at 2,800.

> These numbers were checked by hand in the first days of global. Check the in-game Expedition menu before spending resources.
