## Up to +10 is safe

From +1 to +10 success is guaranteed and the stones can be recovered by extraction. Take every piece you wear to +10.

## +11 to +15 is chance-based

Each failure adds +5% to the next attempt at that level, until it is guaranteed. Failing only costs Kinah and stones: the item does not break or lose a level. The full table is in the odds tool.

+11 and +12 are affordable. From +13 on, the cost per attempt more than doubles and the rate drops.

## A piece's effective level

Enhancement adds item level. On a Unique piece, +10 is worth +20 levels; on an Epic one, +14.

That is why the green comparison arrow misleads: it only looks at base level. A level 70 piece at +10 is worth 90. A new level 86 piece at +0 is worth 86, so it is a **downgrade** until you enhance it.

| Swap | At +0 | At +10 |
|---|---|---|
| Unique 70 +10 for Unique 86 | −4 | +16 |
| Epic 42 +10 for Unique 86 | +30 | +50 |

Replace the slot where rarity also goes up first.

## What carries over on transfer

- **Carries over:** enhancement level and soul binding.
- **Does not:** manastones and Potential.

## After +15: Amplification

A Unique piece at +15 gets a second track, 1 to 5, that adds orange stats. Each attempt needs an Amplify Stone. It only makes sense on a piece you will keep for a long time.
